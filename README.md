## 📖 About This Book

**The Grand Saga of World History** is an ambitious historical knowledge project exploring the complete journey of human civilization — from the origins of humanity to the modern world.

This book explores ancient civilizations, powerful empires, legendary rulers, historic wars, revolutions, religions, cultures, scientific discoveries, technological innovations, and the evolution of modern society.

Its purpose is to help readers understand how humanity evolved, how civilizations developed, how history shaped the present, and what lessons the past offers for the future.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Explore the origins and evolution of humanity.
- Understand the rise and fall of ancient civilizations.
- Discover the stories of great kings and empires.
- Study major wars and historical revolutions.
- Explore the history of Nepal and the world.
- Understand the development of religions and cultures.
- Learn about scientific discoveries and inventions.
- Examine political, social, and economic transformations.
- Connect historical events with the modern world.
